Zoë met Sam on one of The Apps. Or did Sam meet Zoë on one of The Apps? Who knows how it works with those Apps. To be safe, we'd better get both sides of the story: 

## How Sam remembers it

I was in Seattle visiting my sister and nephew, it was early August. It had been a pretty rough year of dating for me: bad dates, not a lot of matches on the apps, etc. I remember telling my friend Belinda, before my first date with Zoë, that if "this one didn't work out" I was going to "take a break" (whatever that means).

I remember thinking that Zoë seemed too good to be matching with me, that we'd probably exchange a few messages and that'd be it. I think she had something in her profile about having a background in zoology (Zoë the zoologist, love it), so I did what I usually could not help myself to do, which was start with the weirdest possible thing I could say. I think I asked her if she had any favourite cryptozoological specimens (you know: bigfoot, chupacabras, that sort of thing).

Fortunately, she was into that sort of bull💩, and we got to talking pretty quickly. (Remember, kids: some things only need to work once.)

The first few months we dated were helped a lot by our shared, weird schedules. I rehearsed with my band into the late hours two or three times a week, and Zoë taught night classes. That meant that we were both "night people," and we would have dinner together at ~10pm on a Tuesday. (Hey, we still do this! Once a night 🦉 always a night 🦉).

## How Zoë remembers it

I met Sam in the Summer of 2019. At the time I’d taken a break from dating. I was never
that keen on it, a person only has so much free time and I was struggling to justify
spending mine with strange men. By August long weekend, after a few long chats with
my close circle, I’d decided to put myself back out there. I redownloaded a dating app,
and it presented me with Sam. Sam on stage, Sam playing hockey, Sam looking into
the camera, an undeniably good-looking guy. His dating profile answers were silly and
intriguing in a way I appreciated, so I figured why not, let’s ‘like’ him.

Generally, these things had a sluggish pace about them, with Sam it was different, he
texted promptly and the conversation flowed easily. Sam is the best text
conversationalist I know. Even in those first hours it was easy. Chatting with Sam didn’t
involve the usual forced banter; he talked to me like I was a friend. He told me about his
weekend visiting his sister in Seattle, how he’d laughed to her that there was no way I
would text back, or that we’d ever meet. I of course proved him wrong; we messaged all
weekend!

We agreed we should meet up and quickly had our first date. I honestly wasn’t too sure
what to think, I learned Sam was involved in multiple music projects, and I hadn’t played
an instrument since I was 15 (flute – badly). My musical ineptitude aside, some part of
me knew that if we didn’t go on a second date I’d miss out on what felt like a budding
friendship. I was right, as we got to know each other a sympatico emerged, we’re night
owls, both like hosting dinner parties, and he was even reading my favourite book when
we met. We went on a weekend trip to the Sunshine Coast, and Sam was happy to nerd
out with me digging around trees looking for mycelium. We travelled together and
moved in together the following spring, building a little garden patch in our new home.
When Sam proposed four summers after we’d met it was an easy ‘yes please!’ on my
part.

The guy is, in my opinion, the best. Sam is great at giving people recommendations of
all sorts. Thanks to him I’ve discovered books by Canadian authors from the 60s and
dined on the best udon soup in Tokyo. I tend to procrastinate and avoid difficult
conversations, whereas Sam addresses things head on. Being with him instills an
underlying sense of calm in me. It’s cheesy, but we balance each other out. Sam is
creative and someone who can make things happen. When he excitedly came to me
with the idea to start a music festival my gut reaction was: are you insane? But then, I knew he could pull it off. Sam has a unique set of traits that make him great
at throwing parties and building community. It’s something I admire, and love taking part
in. Sam is a talented writer, and as I’m sure many of you know, he’s hilarious. He
comes up with witticisms in spades and does impeccable impersonations. The days I
spend with Sam have me rolling my eyes and laughing my ass off. Meeting on a dating
app may not be the most interesting ‘how did you meet story,’ but that’s how it went and
I’m lucky that we did.
